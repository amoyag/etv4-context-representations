# ETV4 regulatory-context protocol v1 — E0 design

Version: E0 v1, 10 November 2026. This specification is fixed before biological model execution. The freeze manifest identifies its exact bytes and dependencies. Source preparation and design are complete only after the recorded review and stage checks; E1 loading/qualification and E2 inference require later activation. The original setup proposal is retained in `handoff/E0-history/protocol-setup.md`.

## Question, evidence and inference

Do frozen model representations distinguish a mutation that changes the reported regulatory response of ETV4 from one that changes DNA affinity while preserving that response? Does homologous conditioning alter ProFam's visible response to the N-terminal sequence beyond the effects of matched generic prefixes? The first question is tested primarily with bidirectional ESM-2 650M. ProFam supplies a complementary, causally limited context test. Neither readout estimates KD or regulatory free energy.

The evidence is Currie et al., NAR 2017, DOI 10.1093/nar/gkx068: original Methods, Figure 2 and Supplementary S1–S3 and S10. Native ETV4 matches human UniProt P43268 sequence version 3. Seven WT boundaries and five mutant genotypes in two contexts yield 17 unique native sequences. Seven WT KD values are tabulated; mutant effects are categorical. No absolute mutant affinities are invented or digitised. Source locators, units, SEM, reported n and assay conditions are retained in `sources/source-audit.md`, `data/derived/construct-registry.tsv` and `measurement-registry.tsv`.

Inputs use the native amino acids, without unreported residual expression scars. The paper describes tag cleavage but does not establish every residual vector residue. NMR ligation-specific changes are not inserted into EMSA constructs. Claims concern representations of the identified native sequences, with retrospective support from the assays; they do not establish exact reagent reconstruction.

## Layers, coordinates and numerical rules

Use post-transformer-block residual outputs before any model-wide final output normalisation. E1 must implement and test this convention, including the last block, instead of assuming a library's hidden-state index means the same operation at every layer. ESM-2 has 33 blocks: primary 22, sensitivity 17 and 33. The released ProFam config has 16 blocks: primary 11, sensitivity 8 and 16. These follow ceil(2L/3), ceil(L/2) and L. No layer is selected using outcomes.

For each native position i, let z(x,i) = h(x,i)/||h(x,i)||2. If a norm is at most 10^-12, fail that readout rather than manufacture a unit vector. Calculate summaries in float64 from recorded activations. Storage/backend precision is fixed by E1 technical qualification, with frozen weights, inference/eval mode, one model at a time and no stochastic generation. Native coordinate i maps to within-target offset i−start, plus the actual prefix/token offset. E1 verifies every residue/token map and BOS/SEP/EOS handling.

ProFam uses `[start-of-document][RAW]`, each full prompt sequence followed by `[SEP]`, then the target and final `[SEP]`, with no padding or truncation. Absolute document positions and causal masking follow the installed released implementation; no custom reset of target positions is allowed. The expected length is 2 + sum(prompt lengths + 1) + target length + 1. These are sequence-level expectations; E1 must verify actual token IDs and positions. Homologous and non-homologous prompts match both member lengths exactly, in both orders, so their target starts coincide. Target N-terminal truncation still changes positions within a pair: length-matched N-shuffled controls measure that alternative explanation.

For each model/layer, E1 sets eta = max(10^-7, 10 × the largest per-residue normalised-vector L2 discrepancy among three identical runs for every artificial qualification input). Use only synthetic inputs with no biological labels, frozen in `design/qualification-inputs.json`: Q1 is 100 residues with empty prefix, Q2 is 272 residues with 477/477 prefixes, Q3 is 484 residues with 510/477 prefixes; Q1_C and Q2_C append ACDEFG for causal checks. Three repeats of five ProFam cases per backend consume 15 passes, or 30 for CPU plus MPS; at most two warmups per backend keep the total at 34, below the 40 reserve. ESM uses Q1/Q2/Q3 targets without prefixes, three repeats per backend (18 for both), plus at most two warmups per backend (22 total, below 30). Other mapping tests are token-only unless charged to the remaining reserve. CPU is the reference backend. If MPS is qualified, include CPU–MPS discrepancies in eta and freeze the chosen backend. The genotype denominator is uninterpretable if at most 4 eta; a difference at most 8 eta cannot support specificity. No numerical threshold is tuned on native ETV4 outputs. A failed causal-invariance check is a technical failure.

## Primary metrics and controls

Write RMS(v) = sqrt(mean_i sum_d v(i,d)^2), over the same fixed mask. For genotype g, q_g = z(FL_g) − z(CID_g). Let M_g = RMS(q_g − q_WT), B = RMS(q_WT) and T_g = M_g/B. Report all three, including undefined values when the numerical floor makes T uninterpretable. The key source contrast is Y401N/Y403G versus E404K and each remaining mutant. There is one regulation-disrupting genotype group; no population-level prediction accuracy, regression, p-value or biological confidence interval is justified.

Also calculate D_g = RMS(z(CID_g) − z(CID_WT)) on the same mask and M_g/D_g when D_g > 4 eta. D_g is the CID-construct mutation baseline (337–436, which includes the CID and is not the isolated structural ETS domain): a large context statistic can be a consequence of a large direct mutation response. The ratio is a prespecified descriptive diagnostic, not a fitted correction. If a control's D is numerically unresolved, report numerator and floor and leave its ratio undefined; do not replace it with a stabilising constant. A missing ratio prevents a claim based on that ratio.

For each of 20 seeds, shuffle FL residues 1–336, preserving length, composition and flank endpoint identities, and use that same prefix for every genotype. Keep each genotype's 337–484 suffix unchanged. Compute q_g^(s), M_g^(s), B^(s) and T_g^(s) against the same native CID inputs. Native and synthetic values remain separate. The distribution tests whether the response requires the native order of the N prefix; it does not measure biological regulation of shuffled proteins. The distal C tail is still present, so this is not a mutant-tail equivalence experiment.

The following conservative descriptive rule fixes the support classification before inference. Let Y be Y401N/Y403G and K be the four other mutant groups. For each g define a numerical sensitivity interval for R_g = M_g/D_g: L_g = max(0, M_g−8 eta)/(D_g+4 eta) and U_g = (M_g+8 eta)/(D_g−4 eta), provided D_g > 4 eta. These are numerical perturbation bounds under the measured eta envelope, not confidence intervals or a proof that all implementation errors are bounded. E1 must reject a backend that fails its measured repeatability/visibility envelope.

Label the primary-layer outcome `source_consistent_native_order_specificity` only if: (1) B > 4 eta and every D_g > 4 eta; (2) M_Y > max_{g in K} M_g + 8 eta; (3) L_Y > max_{g in K} U_g; and (4) native M_Y > max of its 20 shuffled-prefix M_Y values + 8 eta. This intentionally tests the directional hypothesis that the reported regulation-disrupting mutation has a larger, native-order-specific context effect than the four controls after the CID-construct diagnostic. It does not predict a direction in embedding coordinates or a sign of affinity change. The positive label describes this single-protein case correspondence; it does not establish biological causation or exclude mutant-specific distal-tail effects.

If the denominators are unresolved, label the outcome `numerically_unresolved`. If they are resolved but any separation fails or ties, label it `specificity_not_established`, stating the failed condition and raw values; failure does not establish that the model contains no regulatory information. Report M/D components, bounds and the count of 20 shuffle M values at or above native M for every genotype. That count is descriptive, not a population p-value. Apply the same rule at the two fixed sensitivity layers and report agreement/disagreement; they cannot replace a failed primary block. Thus a positive primary outcome with inconsistent sensitivity layers is explicitly layer-dependent. No metric, layer or genotype subset is selected to rescue the interpretation.

For the WT factorial block, J = z(NC) − z(N) − z(C) + z(0); report RMS(J) and its per-residue norm profile. Twenty paired controls use a shuffled 165–336 N flank and independently shuffled 431–436 C flank, preserving each flank's endpoints and composition. For each seed generate N_s, C_s and NC_s with the same component sequences, and calculate J_s. Additional N_s with the native C flank supplies the N-only specificity control. A nonzero J is expected to be possible in a nonlinear network and is not itself biological cooperativity. The biological contrast is independently ln[KD(NC) KD(0)/(KD(N) KD(C))], computed from S3 means; do not compare its sign to a nonnegative representation norm or infer an energy calibration. No error bars are invented from unavailable assay covariance. With only four permutable interior C-flank residues, the fixed 20 seeded draws contain 16 distinct permutations and one native-order draw. Keep duplicate/native draws and ties; do not replace them after inspection. This is a with-replacement computational reference distribution, not 20 unique biological controls.

For ProFam, q_P = z(165–436 under P) − z(337–436 under P), on 337–430. Report RMS(q_P), its sitewise norm profile, and RMS(q_hom − q_nonhom) for each matched set and order. Also report RMS(q_hom − q_homshuffle), RMS(q_P − q_empty) and the within-set order contrast RMS(q_ab − q_ba). These quantities use a fixed model/layer coordinate basis. All five sets and both orders are shown; orders and sets are dependent sensitivity observations. Do not compare raw vector coordinates across different models.

The 20 N-shuffle target controls run under the empty prompt and both orders of set 1 for homologous, non-homologous and composition-shuffled homologous prompts. Compare RMS(q_native) with all 20 RMS(q_Nshuffle) values in each of these seven conditions, plus their sitewise profiles. Set 1 was fixed by seed, not chosen from outcomes. Evidence for sequence-specific native N sensitivity is limited to this tested subset; five-set prompt effects alone do not establish native-order specificity across all prompts. ProFam readouts are descriptive model effects: E2 reports their magnitude relative to numerical error, order variation and the fixed shuffle distributions, without a binary claim that homology improves biological regulation encoding. A generic change under any prefix is insufficient evidence for regulatory information. Imperfect amino-acid composition and taxonomy matching prevents a pure homology-causation claim even if effects persist. ProFam mutant responses under empty prompts remain explicitly secondary and partial-context; they cannot overturn a failed ESM biological comparison.

For ProFam's WT 430/436 causal check, all earlier shared states must agree to the E1 numerical tolerance when a C-terminal extension is appended with identical prefix and positions. J is therefore a software/visibility check in ProFam, not a biological primary endpoint. WT distal-tail and FL comparisons under empty prompts retain the same restriction.

Simple baselines report input length, 20 amino-acid frequencies, charge proxy (K+R−D−E)/length, native-position sequence identity and D_g. Describe these for the exact paired input, separately for domain and flanks. No high-dimensional predictor is fitted to this panel, and residues, shuffles, prompt orders and numerical repeats are not biological replicates.

## Changes from the setup proposal and interpretation boundaries

Before any model outcome, E0 made the following explicit: exact 17 native sequences; categorical mutant endpoints; 88-position genotype mask; ProFam block 11; matched natural prefix lengths; explicit prompt selection/shuffle seeds; layer-output convention; CID-construct response diagnostic; FL shuffled-prefix controls; numerical-floor rule; exact pass list; a deterministic, conservative descriptive support classification. The latter was specified in response to design review before any outcome. These resolve previously open definitions and add controls within the existing question. Optional mutation scores/models/ETV1/5 biological tests are absent. Neither the protein family nor the primary biological question changes.

The directed novelty record is `sources/novelty-check.md`. Prior work already supplies family conditioning and PLM-based allostery analysis. A meaningful contribution depends on a controlled answer to these particular regulatory comparisons. Architectural blindness, changing embeddings or an unsuccessful installation are insufficient. No first-in-field claim, causal biological mechanism, predictive affinity model or preprint readiness is established by E0. Negative biological/model results remain reportable if the control and numerical sensitivity support their scope.

After freeze, corrections require a new version and preservation of the old manifest/receipt. Any outcome-informed change is exploratory and cannot silently replace the primary analysis. Models stay frozen. No SAE training, wet-lab work, PPAR/PDZ execution, paper/application writing, external message, upload or submission is included in E0.
