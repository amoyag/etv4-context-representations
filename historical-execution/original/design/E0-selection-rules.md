# E0 selection rules, recorded before any model execution

Date: 10 November 2026. This is sequence curation, not a biological model run.

The focal sequence is the 484-residue human ETV4 sequence in Currie Supplementary Figure S1, reconciled against UniProt P43268 sequence version 3. Native sequence coordinates are inclusive and one-based. The model inputs omit expression-vector residues: the paper reports His-tag cleavage but does not specify the residual EMSA cloning scar. NMR ligation scars are not transferred to EMSA constructs.

Homologue candidates are the canonical sequences returned by the recorded UniProt query for reviewed ETV1 or ETV5 entries. Require 400–550 residues, the standard 20 amino acids, no fragment annotation, at least 60% identity across the best ungapped 82-residue match to ETV4 339–420, and less than 90% full-length global identity to ETV4. Exclude exact full-length duplicates and an exactly identical focal ETS segment. Full-length near duplication and domain similarity are separate variables: the close ETV1/5 domains are intended conditioning information, not independent regulatory labels. Use global alignment with match 1, mismatch 0, gap open −2 and extension −0.5; divide identical aligned letters by the longer full sequence. Record all candidates and exclusions.

Generate the unordered pairs of retained accessions in lexical order; sample five distinct pairs without replacement with Python Random seed 20261110. Within-pair sequences differ; reuse between pairs is allowed and reported. Run each pair in both orders. This is a small, dependent prompt panel, not five independent biological replicates.

The non-homologue pool is the first 500 returned reviewed human nuclear proteins of length 400–550 in the recorded query, excluding annotated ETS-family entries. It is a frozen convenience pool, not a representative proteome sample. Additionally exclude ETS annotation in the entry, nonstandard residues, fragments, and any best ungapped focal-domain match of 30% identity or more. Match each homologue to an exactly equal-length sequence; select the distinct control pair that minimizes summed L1 distance between amino-acid frequency vectors, using exact rational arithmetic on integer counts/sequence length, with accession tuple as a deterministic tie-break. Controls may recur across sets. Save composition, charge and taxonomy mismatch; length matching alone cannot isolate homology as a cause. Composition-shuffled homologous prompts provide an additional exactly composition/length-matched model control.

Do not broaden the family or relax eligibility based on model outcomes. A failure to produce five pairs or exact length matches stops the design for correction before inference. Exclude optional ETV1/5 biological measurements from this MVP: the source's human ETV1 is 479 residues whereas the retrieved canonical entry is 477. Those measurements need separate isoform reconciliation. Their exclusion does not affect use of identified natural ETV1/5 sequences as unlabeled conditioning inputs.

Seeds are 20261110 for pair sampling; 11001–11020 for N-terminal flank shuffles (165–336); 12001–12020 for the paired C flank (431–436); 13001–13020 for FL N-terminal shuffles (1–336); and 14000 + 10 × set + member_index (set = 1…5, member_index = 0 or 1 before order reversal) for prompt shuffles. Python `random.Random(seed).shuffle` acts on amino-acid lists. N/C shuffles retain the first and last residue of each flank and permute the interior. FL shuffled prefixes are shared across genotypes for each seed. Every synthetic input remains labeled model-only. No experimental endpoint is inherited by a synthetic sequence.
